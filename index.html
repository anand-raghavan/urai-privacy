Urai — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 5, 2025

Urai is a visual language learning app designed for autistic children. We take your child's privacy seriously.

No Data Collected

Urai does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data (progress, settings, session history) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

No Internet Required

Urai works entirely offline. No network connection is required for any feature.

No Accounts

Urai does not require or support user accounts, logins, or registration of any kind.

No Analytics or Advertising

Urai contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

COPPA Compliance

Urai is designed for children under 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not collect any personal information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: urai.feedback@gmail.com
