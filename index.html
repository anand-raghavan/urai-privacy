Urai — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 5, 2026

Urai is a visual language learning app designed for autistic children. We take your child's privacy seriously.

No Data Collected or Transmitted

Urai does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data — including session history, progress, and settings — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Local Progress Tracking

Urai tracks your child's language interactions (such as which items were tapped and how often) solely to power the in-app Parent Progress view and to unlock new scenes as the child explores. This data exists only on your device and is never shared with anyone.

No Internet Required

Urai works entirely offline. No network connection is required for any feature.

No Accounts

Urai does not require or support user accounts, logins, or registration of any kind.

No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

Urai contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

COPPA Compliance

Urai is designed for children under 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not collect any personal information from children.

Contact
